Sourcing Helper finds the people behind an internship posting, helps you write to them, and reminds you to follow up. It's free, and works without any API keys.

  1. Pin it.Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Sourcing Helper. Or open it any time with Alt + Shift + S.
  2. Open a job posting on LinkedIn, then click the icon.The Job tab shows where to start: the company's recruiters, people posting that they're hiring, and the team you'd join.
  3. Open someone's profile and click the icon again.The Person tab finds their likely email and has Write message: a ready-to-send note with the role, company, and Job ID filled in.
  4. Click "Mark as messaged".You'll get a reminder to follow up in 5 days. Most replies come from the follow-up.
  5. Looking for internships to apply to?The Find tab searches the job boards companies post on, and shows what was posted in the last 48 hours.
One minute that makes every message better: open Settings and fill in About you (name, school, year, major, one line about something you built). The templates use it so you never send a message with a blank in it.

Optional keys in Settings add more: Hunter or a verifier to confirm emails, Claude to personalize messages. Every email that isn't confirmed is marked as a guess, so check it before you send.

Something broken? Use Report a problem at the bottom of the popup.
